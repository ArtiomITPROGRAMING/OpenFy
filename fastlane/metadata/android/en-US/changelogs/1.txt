OpenFy v1.0.0-beta01:
• 100% FOSS & Offline-first architecture (zero telemetry, no accounts).
• Modern Material 3 UI with dynamic colors and pure OLED dark mode.
• P2P Local Share & QR Scanner for offline playlist exchange.
• Real-time synced lyrics (.lrc) with auto-discovery and embedded tag parser.
• Hardware 5-band DSP Equalizer with Bass Boost, Virtualizer, and Reverb presets.
• Sleep Timer with smooth countdown and volume fade-out.
• Distraction-free Car Mode with oversized controls and vector icons.
