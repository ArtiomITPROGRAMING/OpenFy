OpenFy is a modern, privacy-focused, 100% offline-first music player crafted with Jetpack Compose and AndroidX Media3 (ExoPlayer). It provides an exceptional local audio experience without telemetry, tracking, or cloud dependencies.

✨ Key Features:

🎵 Offline-First Audio Engine
- Crystal-clear playback powered by ExoPlayer and Media3.
- Full format support: MP3, FLAC, AAC, OGG, WAV, M4A, Opus.
- Seamless gapless playback and smart audio focus handling.
- Android Auto support for in-car dashboard navigation.

🎤 Real-time Karaoke & Lyrics (.LRC)
- Automatic discovery of .lrc files located next to your audio tracks.
- Native tag extractor for embedded lyrics (ID3v2 USLT, FLAC VORBIS_COMMENT, MP4 ©lyr).
- Smart charset detection supporting UTF-8, UTF-16, and Windows-1251.
- Synchronized smooth karaoke scrolling with manual line seeking.

🎚️ 5-Band DSP Hardware Equalizer
- 5 frequency bands (60Hz, 230Hz, 910Hz, 3.6kHz, 14kHz).
- Built-in sound enhancement: Bass Boost, 3D Virtualizer, and Reverb presets.
- Persistent preset saving and instant audio effect restoration.

🚗 Distraction-Free Car Mode
- Clean, high-contrast user interface designed for driving.
- Large interactive buttons with standard Material vector icons (no emojis).
- Quick driving playlists: Favorites, Travel Mix, and Recent tracks.

🎨 Dynamic Material Design & Customization
- Dynamic colors extracted from album artwork with Material You palette.
- Pure OLED Dark Theme for maximum battery efficiency.
- Sleek Floating Dock Capsule navigation in the now-playing screen.
- Gestures: swipe cover to skip tracks, double-tap to pause/resume.
- Sleep timer with configurable countdown and fade-out.

🔒 100% Free, Open Source & Private
- No ads, no analytics, no trackers.
- Works entirely offline without requiring internet access for playback.
